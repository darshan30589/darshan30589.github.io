01 — About Me

Data Engineer. AI Builder.
Platform Owner.

I'm a Lead Data Engineer based in Dubai, UAE with 13+ years of experience designing and running enterprise-scale data platforms. I specialise in the full data journey — from ingestion and wrangling through to ML model deployment and governed analytics.

At MoHRE (UAE Ministry of Human Resources & Emiratisation) I lead Dataiku DSS platform delivery — building production AI/ML pipelines including the Dynamic Risk Matrix (DRM), which consolidates 35+ business rules per company into a single risk score, and the ISIC-4 economic classification pipeline using Dataiku and OpenAI.

Previously at ClearPeaks SL I delivered data engineering for Crown Prince Court (Abu Dhabi), ADNOC, and American Tower Corporation (US). My career spans UAE Government, Healthcare, Energy, US Telecom, and KSA Health — cross-domain depth built across six organisations over 13 years.

I hold a SnowPro Core Certification, Oracle Cloud Architect, and multiple Informatica and Dataiku certifications. I'm actively expanding in Azure and deepening AI/GenAI pipeline delivery.
